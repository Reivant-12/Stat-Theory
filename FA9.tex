\section{Problem 1: Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit
Test}\label{problem-1-chi-square-goodness-of-fit-test}

We test whether the coins are fair.

\subsection{Step 1: Hypotheses}\label{step-1-hypotheses}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \(H_0\): Coins are fair (observed = expected)
\item
  \(H_a\): Coins are not fair
\end{itemize}
